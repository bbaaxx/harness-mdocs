# Plan-Gated Autonomous Run Behavior — Specification 1

## Status

Processed and reconciled with specification 2. Final architecture: `architecture/extensible-harness-agents`. Hierarchy detail: `architecture/hierarchical-run-orchestration-spec-2`.

## User Intent

Mdocs requires concrete execution plan before goal-oriented work. Agent review may establish plan quality, but human approves exact plan revision. Human then selects:

- **Milestone mode:** execute and verify one milestone, checkpoint, summarize, return control.
- **Autonomous mode:** pursue full approved plan as persistent goal, continuing across successful milestones without polite stops.

## Findings Preserved

- `/goal` provides continuation and transcript-evidence evaluation, not plan approval or permissions.
- Dynamic workflows move orchestration into inspectable script and expose progress controls, but native approval varies by permission mode and resume may replay work.
- Ultracode demonstrates trusted human-origin activation; injected text cannot authorize autonomous Run.
- Ralplan separates reviewed plan from human execution consent.
- Ralph supplies story criteria, fresh evidence, persistence, bounded retries, final review, and explicit polite-stop avoidance.
- Ultragoal combines aggregate goal with milestone ledger/checkpoints.
- Team preserves one lead and bounded workers; Ultrawork is parallelism only.
- Exactly one primary continuation authority may exist per Run.

## Required Distinctions

1. Plan quality review.
2. Human plan approval bound to immutable plan/graph digest.
3. Separate trusted human mode selection.
4. Action permissions and autonomy envelope.
5. Global continuation authority.
6. Milestone evidence.
7. Whole-goal integration and final verdict.

Mutable progress stays outside approved plan. Autonomous continuation confers no authority. Plan amendment invalidates approval. Completion requires all approved milestones plus integration/regression checks, final review, and no unresolved outcomes.

## Autonomous Pause Conditions

Return control only for explicit human control, missing/denied approval, plan amendment, fundamental blocker, uncertain external outcome, workspace conflict, budget/no-progress exhaustion, policy/checkpoint/audit failure, or unsafe surface degradation.

Successful milestone, ordinary verification, reviewer approval, progress summary, or discovery of next approved milestone are not pause conditions in autonomous mode.
